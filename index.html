Your footage, live from anywhere.

DDraft keeps heavy camera files on the Macs and drives that already hold them — and makes them live-accessible to your whole team from any location. No terabyte uploads. No cloud copies. Real bytes of the real file.

What DDraft does

  • Live media accessRAW, 4K/8K and multi-terabyte camera footage stays on your own hardware, yet opens instantly in the browser, in Finder, and inside DaVinci Resolve — from anywhere with good internet.
  • Peer-to-peer by designYour Macs talk directly to each other. Your data never passes through our storage — we move your bytes, we don't keep them.
  • Collaborative timelineLay out, trim and review together in real time, then hand off to DaVinci Resolve for the finish.
  • DDraft Agent for macOSA small desktop app that serves your media, mounts remote folders as a local disk, and keeps everything in sync. Apple silicon native.
